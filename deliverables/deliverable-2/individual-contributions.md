# 1.0 Team Contribution and Collaboration 

## 1.1 Team Organization and Workflow

During Deliverable 2, the team worked collaboratively while dividing responsibilities according to major project components. To improve efficiency, the project was separated into two main sections: precipitation data processing and TTC delay data processing. Consistent communication through Discord and iMessage allowed the team to coordinate dataset structures, standardize column names, and ensure compatibility between the independently developed workflows. Regular discussions were held to align preprocessing methods, particularly because TTC delay data before and after 2025 required different treatment due to changes in delay classification systems. The team also collaborated on project planning, working through implementation challenges, and integrating results into one workflow.
